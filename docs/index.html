The Chamorro Lab is a quantum materials research group in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. We grow high-quality single crystals of candidate quantum materials, spanning elements across the periodic table, and measure them at low temperature. Our work sits at the intersection of materials science, inorganic and solid state chemistry, and condensed matter physics.
