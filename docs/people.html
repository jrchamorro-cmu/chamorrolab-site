Juan studied chemistry at Johns Hopkins University from 2013 to 2021, first as an undergraduate and then as a PhD student, and was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of California, Santa Barbara, in the Materials Department and the Materials Research Laboratory from 2021 to 2024. In his free time he enjoys travel and reading.
