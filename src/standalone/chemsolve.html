ChemSolve

Chamorro Research Group copy of ChemSolve by the McQueen Lab. Calculations run in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Target Formula: The target formula you want to make
Starting Materials: The metals, oxides, carbonates, nitrates, etc. to start with.
DO NOT Balance: These elements/compounds are ignored for atom-balance purposes. CO3=O means, e.g., that Na2CO3 is treated as Na2O and NO3=O means, e.g., that NaNO3 is treated as NaO. O means that oxygen can equilibrate with the atmosphere.
Quantity / mg: The total quantity of , in milligrams.

Notes:
    
  
  • NaNO3 and Ba(NO3)2 are recognized as nitrates whereas ZrNbO3N
      is treated as an oxynitride. To force recognition of Ba(NO3)2 as an oxynitride you would write BaN2O6 instead.
      To force recognition of ZrNbO3N as a nitrate, you would write ZrNbNO3.
    • 
  
  • Generally items on the do not balance list should be ordered from most to least specific.
    • 
  
  • For do not balance items, only individual elements may be directly ignored. Hydrates
      (e.g. Ni(NO3)2(H2O)6) are not handled correctly by this version: the hydrate is reported as not used.
    • 
  
  • Isotopes can be specified. For example, you can target Sm(B_11)5B_10 , with Sm, B_11, and B_10 as starting materials.
    •
