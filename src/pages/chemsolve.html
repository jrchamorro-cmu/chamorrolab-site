Notes

  
    
    
  • NaNO3 and Ba(NO3)2 are read as nitrates; ZrNbO3N is read as an oxynitride. Write BaN2O6 to force an oxynitride reading of Ba(NO3)2, or ZrNbNO3 to force a nitrate reading of ZrNbO3N.
    • 
    
  • Order the do-not-balance list from most to least specific.
    • 
    
  • Only single elements can be ignored directly. Hydrates such as Ni(NO3)2(H2O)6 are not handled correctly yet: the calculation reports the hydrate as not used.
    • 
    
  • Isotopes use an underscore. For example, target Sm(B_11)5B_10 from Sm, B_11 and B_10.
